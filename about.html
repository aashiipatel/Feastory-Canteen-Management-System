🍔 About Us – Meet Feastory!

At Feastory, we’re serving up more than just food—we’re serving convenience, connection, and care. We are a smart platform built to simplify how canteens and students interact. Canteens can log in, update their daily menus, and receive real-time orders from students. No more long queues, confusion, or missed meals—just a smooth, easy-to-use system that keeps everyone fed and happy.
